# (dev)Modcraft artwork

The site uses `pepe-crowd-v1.png`, generated with the built-in image generation
tool. The title and controls are real HTML, kept separate from the illustration.
Geist fonts are bundled locally under the accompanying SIL Open Font License.

Design: a dense, irregular rectangular crowd surrounds one quiet central menu.
Ice blue `#dcebef`, frog green `#4c8c3f`, dark outline `#203426`, pale highlight
`#f5ffe9`, and warm yellow `#f0c861` complement the illustration. Geist Semibold
carries the large centered wordmark; Geist Regular keeps forms readable. Chunky
beveled buttons echo the game's controls. No floating cards or extra decoration
compete with the crowd. On narrow screens, the side crowds crop to preserve a
usable central form, with the top and bottom Pepes still visible.

The prompt below was used verbatim in built-in mode (no API/CLI fallback).

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: production website background illustration, wide landscape 16:9, 2048x1152 composition.
Primary request: a comically excessive crowd of 45 to 60 different Pepe the Frog meme characters crowded around the edges of the image, forming an irregular softly rounded RECTANGULAR ring around a large completely empty middle where a website's title and login buttons will be placed.
Subject and variety: unmistakable classic Pepe faces with green skin, bulging expressive eyes and broad frog mouths. Many distinct sizes, poses, outfits and moods: tiny baby Pepe in a onesie, galaxy-brain genius Pepe, idea Pepe with a glowing lightbulb, laptop coder Pepe, builder Pepe with a voxel grass block, wizard Pepe, headphone gamer Pepe, sleepy coffee Pepe, artist Pepe, chef Pepe, crying laughing Pepe, smug Pepe, surprised Pepe, little shy Pepe, proud Pepe, astronaut Pepe, scientist Pepe. Mix large foreground characters and silly tiny ones peeking between them. Dense layered crowd spilling off every outer edge, especially corners. Fun absurd chaotic internet-meme energy, not sinister.
Composition/framing: absolutely NO characters or objects in the central 44% of image width and central 76% of image height. This clean space is centered and shaped like a tall broad rounded rectangle, with smooth slightly wobbly organic edges. The crowd forms a dense irregular rectangular wreath around it, NOT a perfect circle or oval. Keep the central empty zone large enough for a heading and a vertically stacked form. There is no actual frame line around the empty region. Overlapping Pepes run along both sides and across the top and bottom, 2 to 3 layers deep, balancing asymmetrically. Large silly expressive faces readable at website size.
Style/medium: lovingly hand-drawn 2D internet meme cartoon collage, bold dark expressive outlines, flat colors with small cel-shaded details; clean high-quality illustration with tactile hand-drawn irregularity.
Scene/backdrop: uniform soft light icy blue (#dcebef) behind the crowd and throughout the completely blank central space.
Constraints: NO text, NO lettering, NO logo, NO buttons, NO UI, NO watermark. No political imagery. All characters stay outside the reserved center. Full bleed landscape illustration.
```
